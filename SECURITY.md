# Security policy

## Scope

This policy covers:

- the forks published by keptsec (the repositories in the keptsec GitHub organization and the packages released from them),
- the website at keptsec.com,
- our build, test, signing and release pipeline.

For a vulnerability in the original upstream project that our fork has not changed, please also tell the upstream maintainers, if the project still accepts reports.

## How to report

1. **Preferred: GitHub private vulnerability reporting.** Open the Security tab of the affected repository and click "Report a vulnerability". If no single repository fits, report it at https://github.com/keptsec/.github/security/advisories/new.
2. **Without a GitHub account:** email support@keptsec.com with a subject line starting with "SECURITY".

Please do not report security problems in public issues, pull requests or discussions.

Include, where you can:

- the affected package or component and its version,
- the steps to reproduce,
- the impact you expect.

Do not include other people's personal data, or credentials beyond what is needed to show the problem.

## What happens next

We review every report and coordinate the fix and its disclosure with you in the private advisory or by email. We do not promise a response time. When a fix ships, the release, its advisory and its attestation entry on keptsec.com say what changed.

## Good-faith research

If you act in good faith, follow our Acceptable Use Policy (https://keptsec.com/aup/), do not access or keep other people's data beyond what is needed to show the problem, do not degrade the service, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it, we will not pursue legal action against you for that research.

## No bug bounty

We do not run a paid bug bounty and do not pay for reports.
